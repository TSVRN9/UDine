# iPhone tester checklist: hall menu

You are testing one part of the app: the dining hall menu and the plate. The app opens straight onto it.
Use your thumbs the way you normally would. Do not hurry; slow, deliberate moves are fine, but also try one quick flick for each gesture.

**How to report:** for every step, record your screen (Control Centre, Screen Recording) while you do it. Send one recording per step, named with the step number. If a step felt wrong in any way, say so even if you cannot say why. "It felt heavy", "it jumped" and "it stuttered" are all useful.

**Every step below should feel smooth, with your finger staying attached to whatever you are moving.** Anything that lags behind your finger, jumps, flickers or blanks out is a problem: report it.

## 1. Open a hall

Tap a dining hall on the Halls tab.
- Correct: the hall's photo grows into the full menu page in one continuous motion. Nothing flashes or jumps.
- Report: a recording of the open, and anything that looked broken.

## 2. Close a hall, three ways

Open a hall three times and close it a different way each time.

a. Tap Back at the top left.
- Correct: the page shrinks back into the hall's photo on the Halls tab.

b. Drag the page down from the top, slowly, and let go partway. Then do it again and let go near the bottom.
- Correct: the page follows your finger. Letting go early springs it back open; letting go late closes it.

c. Swipe from the very left edge of the screen to the right, slowly. Do it once and stop halfway, then lift your finger.
- Correct: the page follows your finger. Stopping halfway and lifting either closes it or springs it back, but it never gets stuck half way.

- Report: one recording per way, and tell me which of them did not work.

## 3. Swipe between meals

Open a hall that has more than one meal (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner). Swipe left and right across the menu.
- Correct: the menu slides with your finger, the meal name at the top changes to match, and the list starts at the top for the new meal.
- Report: a recording that includes a fast flick and a slow drag, and any moment the meal name and the list disagreed.

## 4. Jump to a station

Tap a station name in the row above the menu. Then scroll the menu by hand and watch that row.
- Correct: tapping scrolls the menu to that station and the row follows. Scrolling by hand moves the highlighted station along with the list.
- Report: a recording, and any station that landed in the wrong place.

## 5. Change the day

Tap the day at the top and choose another day, then another.
- Correct: the menu changes to that day with no empty flash, and the day label updates.
- Report: a recording of at least two changes, and any blank screen you saw.

## 6. Open the hours

Tap the hours line under the hall's name.
- Correct: a sheet slides up showing the week's hours, already scrolled to the day you are looking at. Drag it down to close it.
- Report: a recording of opening it, scrolling it, and closing it.

## 7. Open a dish, drag it, dismiss it

Tap a dish.
- Correct: a sheet rises with the dish's details. Drag it up and down partway and let go; it settles in place. Drag it down hard, or tap outside it, to close it.
- Report: a recording of open, drag and close, and anything under the sheet that moved when it should not have.

## 8. Add, half and remove

On a dish, tap to add it to your plate. Tap again to step it down to a half. Tap again to remove it.
- Correct: the plate bar at the bottom appears with the first add, its count and calories change with every tap, and it goes away when the plate is empty.
- Report: a recording of the three taps, and any tap that did nothing or counted twice.

## 9. Drag the plate sheet

With a few dishes on the plate, tap the plate bar to open it, then drag the sheet slowly to each resting height: small, tall, and back down.
- Correct: it stops at set heights, follows your finger the whole way, and the list inside it scrolls without moving the sheet unless you are at the top of the list.
- Report: a recording of every height, and any place the sheet fought your finger.

## 10. Log, then Undo

Open the plate sheet and tap Log. Then tap Undo straight away.
- Correct: logging empties the plate and shows an Undo. Tapping Undo puts exactly the same dishes back.
- Report: a recording, and tell me if the plate came back different.

## 11. Write meals to Apple Health

On You, with Track what I eat on, tap Write to Apple Health. Allow it in the sheet iOS shows. Then log a plate, change a quantity in that meal in the log, delete the meal, and tap Undo on the delete. After each step open the Health app, Browse, Nutrition (Dietary Energy, Protein, Carbohydrates, Total Fat, Sodium).
- Correct: logging adds one entry per nutrient at the meal's time; changing a quantity replaces those entries with the new amounts (never two copies); deleting removes them; Undo brings them back. Turning the row off leaves the entries already there alone and adds none after. Denying the sheet leaves the check empty. Switching UDine's access off in Settings, Health, Data Access, then reopening UDine, shows the row as not allowed.
- Report: a screenshot of Health after each step, and whether any entry stayed behind or doubled after an edit or a delete.

## Finally

Tell me anything else that felt less smooth than the apps you use every day, even if it is not in this list.
