UDine Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

UDine is a menu browser and calorie/macro tracker for UMass Dining. This section describes what the current version of the app does with your data.

No accounts

This version of the app has no accounts and no sign-in. There is nothing to register, and nothing tying activity in the app to your identity.

What stays on your device

Your food log, calorie and macro history, and any dish comparisons you make all stay stored locally on your device. We never see this data, and the app never sends it to our servers.

What the app fetches from the network

Dining hall menus, hours, and campus dining events are fetched directly from UMass Dining's own public systems. The app also fetches a shared, public dish-nutrition dataset from our server to make food search faster. That lookup is read-only and doesn't include any information about you or your device.

Exporting your data

You can export your on-device food log and macro history as a JSON or CSV file at any time from within the app.

Camera and notifications

The app does not use your camera and does not request notification permissions in this version.

Children's privacy

UDine is intended for UMass Amherst dining hall visitors and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

Future versions of the app may add accounts, friends, or notifications. If that happens, this page will be updated to describe what changes before those features ship.
