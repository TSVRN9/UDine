UDine Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

UDine is a menu browser and food log for UMass Dining. This page describes what the current version of the app does with your data.

No accounts

UDine has no accounts and no sign-in. Nothing you do in the app is tied to your name, email address, or any other identity.

What stays on your device

The meals you log, their calories and nutrients, the foods you add or save (Your foods), your favorite dishes, and your settings are stored only on your device. UDine never sends them to us or to anyone else, and we can't see them.

UDine also keeps copies of dining menus and café menu PDFs on your device so it can show them without a connection.

What the app downloads

Menus, dining hours, café menus, and campus dining events (with their images) come directly from UMass Dining's public website. UMass Dining receives these requests the way any website does, and its own policies apply to them.

To show how busy a dining hall usually is, the app asks our server for that hall's typical crowd levels. The request names the dining hall and the day of the week, and nothing else. Our server is hosted by Supabase, which, like any web host, keeps standard request logs that include your device's IP address. We don't use those logs to identify anyone.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Searching for foods

When you search for a food that isn't on a dining menu, UDine sends the words you typed, and nothing else, to two public food databases: Open Food Facts and the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests. UDine doesn't send these searches while food tracking is off or while your phone is offline.

If you then choose to search UMass Dining for the food, the app sends the words you typed to our server, which looks them up on UMass Dining's public nutrition site. A dish it finds is added to a public list of UMass dish names and their nutrition. For a search that finds nothing, the server keeps the words searched and the time, so it doesn't repeat the same lookup. Neither is linked to you or your device.

Sharing and links

UDine shares something only when you ask it to. When you export your data or share an event flyer, your device's share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes. Directions open in your maps app, and event links open in your browser. Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies.

Exporting and deleting your data

Export my data saves your food log, your favorites, and Your foods as JSON and CSV files. You can edit or delete any logged meal, and any food in Your foods, in the app. Uninstalling UDine deletes everything it stored on your device.

Permissions

This version of UDine doesn't use your camera or your location.

If you turn on Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) on the You tab, UDine asks your phone for permission to write nutrition, and then saves each meal you log there: when you ate it, its name (such as "Lunch at Worcester"), and its calories, fat, saturated fat, trans fat (Android only), cholesterol, sodium, carbohydrates, fiber, sugar and protein. Editing or deleting a meal in UDine updates or removes that record. UDine only writes: it never reads anything from Apple Health or Health Connect, and nothing it writes passes through UDine's server. To update its records, UDine keeps the IDs of what it wrote, on your device only. Records already written stay in Apple Health or Health Connect until you delete them there.

If you turn on Notify me for a favorite dish, UDine asks your phone for permission to show notifications. The alerts come through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. While Notify me is on, your device tells Google which dishes it follows, as a coded name for each favorited dish. Anyone with the public menu can decode those names, so Google can tell which dishes your device follows. UDine's server doesn't learn which dishes you follow, or anything else about you or your device. Turning Notify me off stops the alerts and removes those follows. UDine doesn't contact Google for this until you turn Notify me on.

Children's privacy

UDine is intended for UMass Amherst dining hall visitors and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of UDine changes what it collects or shares, this page will be updated before that version is released. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
