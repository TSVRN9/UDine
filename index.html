UDine Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

UDine is a menu browser and food log for UMass Dining. This page describes what the current version of the app does with your data.

No accounts

UDine has no accounts and no sign-in. Nothing you do in the app is tied to your name, email address, or any other identity.

What stays on your device

The meals you log, their calories and nutrients, the foods you add yourself, your favorite dishes, and your settings are stored only on your device. UDine never sends them to us or to anyone else, and we can't see them.

UDine also keeps copies of dining menus and café menu PDFs on your device so it can show them without a connection.

What the app downloads

Menus, dining hours, café menus, and campus dining events (with their images) come directly from UMass Dining's public website. UMass Dining receives these requests the way any website does, and its own policies apply to them.

To show how busy a dining hall usually is, the app asks our server for that hall's typical crowd levels. The request names the dining hall and the day of the week, and nothing else. Our server is hosted by Supabase, which, like any web host, keeps standard request logs that include your device's IP address. We don't use those logs to identify anyone.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Sharing and links

UDine shares something only when you ask it to. When you export your data or share an event flyer, your device's share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes. Directions open in your maps app, and event links open in your browser. Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies.

Exporting and deleting your data

Export my data saves your food log, your favorites, and the foods you've added as JSON and CSV files. You can edit or delete any logged meal in the app. Uninstalling UDine deletes everything it stored on your device.

Permissions

This version of UDine doesn't use your camera or your location, doesn't send notifications, and doesn't connect to Apple Health or Health Connect.

Children's privacy

UDine is intended for UMass Amherst dining hall visitors and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of UDine changes what it collects or shares, this page will be updated before that version is released. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
